How AM Synthesis Works

In AM (Amplitude Modulation) synthesis, the amplitude (loudness) of a carrier wave is varied by a modulator oscillator.

output(t) = [1 + m · sin(2π·f_m·t)] · A · sin(2π·f_c·t) where f_c = carrier frequency f_m = modulator frequency m = modulation depth (0 = off, 1 = full AM)

At sub-audio rates (f_m < 20 Hz) this creates tremolo — a rhythmic volume wobble. As f_m enters the audio range, sidebands appear at f_c ± f_m, creating ring modulation with metallic tones.

Watch the waveform envelope change shape, and the spectrum grow sidebands as you sweep the modulator frequency up with a rightward movement.
