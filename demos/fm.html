How FM Synthesis Works

In FM (Frequency Modulation) synthesis, one oscillator (the modulator) continuously varies the frequency of another (the carrier).

output(t) = A · sin(2π·f_c·t + β · sin(2π·f_m·t)) where f_c = carrier frequency f_m = modulator frequency β = modulation index (timbre)

The output contains sidebands at frequencies f_c ± n·f_m (n = 1, 2, 3 …). Their amplitudes follow Bessel functions. As β grows, energy spreads into higher sidebands — watch the spectrum light up progressively.

The C:M ratio determines whether sidebands land on harmonic multiples of a common pitch (musical) or off-grid (bell-like, metallic).
