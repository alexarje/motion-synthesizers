Motion Synthesizers

Control sound with your body. Five browser-based synthesizers driven by webcam motion — each demonstrating a different synthesis technique.

Choose a Synthesis Type

🌈

Additive Synthesis

Build complex tones by layering pure sine waves (harmonics). Any sound can be created by summing enough sine waves.

Harmonics Fourier Timbre
🌊

Subtractive Synthesis

Carve away frequencies from a harmonically rich wave using resonant filters. The classic synth sound.

Filters Resonance Sawtooth
📡

FM Synthesis

Modulate the frequency of one oscillator with another. Small changes in the modulation index produce dramatic timbral shifts.

Sidebands Modulation Index C:M Ratio
📻

AM Synthesis

Modulate the amplitude of a carrier wave. At low rates it creates tremolo; at audio rates it creates ring modulation.

Tremolo Ring Mod Sidebands
🎸

Physical Modelling

Simulate a plucked string using the Karplus-Strong algorithm — a feedback delay line that mimics a real vibrating string.

Karplus-Strong Delay Line String

How It Works

01

Grant Camera Access

Each demo asks for webcam access. If declined, you can use your mouse to control the synth instead.

02

Move to Control

Left/right movement maps to one parameter; up/down to another; overall motion intensity to a third.

03

See & Hear

Watch the real-time audio visualisation and parameter display while you explore each synthesis type.
